Designed and implemented a USB host controller in SystemVerilog, using finite-state machines to manage read/write transactions, packet sequencing, ACK/NAK handshaking, retransmissions, and timeout-based error recovery. Integrated NRZI encoding/decoding, bit stuffing, and CRC validation, and verified functionality through fault-injection simulations.

**Technologies:** SystemVerilog, Digital Design, Synopsys VCS
