Designed and implemented a synthesizable packet-switched Network-on-Chip in SystemVerilog, including multi-port routers, packet serialization/deserialization, FIFO buffering, handshake-based flow control, destination-based routing, and round-robin arbitration. Verified the design with directed, stress, fairness, and performance testbenches, including tests of concurrency and buffer-management edge cases.
