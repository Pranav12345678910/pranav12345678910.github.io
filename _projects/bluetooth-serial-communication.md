Designed and implemented a serial communication system in SystemVerilog on an FPGA, using finite-state machines to receive, decode, and transmit data. Integrated SECDED error correction to correct single-bit errors and detect double-bit errors, along with dynamic bit synchronization to accommodate variations in transmission rate.
