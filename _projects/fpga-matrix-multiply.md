Designed a 100 MHz pipelined matrix-multiply accelerator
on a Spartan-7 FPGA using 32 parallel DSP multipliers,
dual-port BRAMs, and a multi-stage adder tree, meeting
post-route timing constraints.

**Technologies:** FPGA, Digital Design
